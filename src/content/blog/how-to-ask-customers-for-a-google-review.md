Asking for a review shouldn't feel like begging, and it shouldn't feel like an imposition on a customer's day either. The businesses that build up a steady stream of Google reviews aren't the ones with the cleverest script — they're the ones that ask **every** customer, **every** time, in a way that takes them twenty seconds and costs them nothing.

This post will cover: the right moment to ask, what to actually say (and what to avoid — anything that looks like an incentive puts you on the wrong side of Google's review guidelines), and why consistency matters more than cleverness.
