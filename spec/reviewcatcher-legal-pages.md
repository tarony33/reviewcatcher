## Privacy Policy

**Last updated: [date]**

This policy explains how ReviewCatcher ("we", "us") handles personal data. It covers two different situations, because our role changes depending on whose data it is.

**1. Who we are**
ReviewCatcher is a business registered in Ireland ([Registered Business Name / RBN number]), based in Rush, Co. Dublin. For questions about this policy or your data, contact us at support@reviewcatcher.ie or [phone].

**2. Two roles — please read this part**

- **When you contact us or use this website** (you're a business owner, a lead, or a site visitor), we are the **data controller** — we decide how your data is used, and this policy applies to you directly.
- **When we send review requests to a client's customers**, we are only the **data processor** — the business that hired us is the controller of that data. If you received a review request or reminder text and want your details removed, reply STOP to the message or contact the business that serves you; you can also contact us and we'll act on their instructions. See our [Data Processing Agreement] for how we protect that data.

**3. What we collect (as controller)**

- **Enquiry / contact data:** name, business name, email, phone number and anything you tell us when you contact us or book a call.
- **Client data:** the information needed to provide and bill for the service.
- **Website data:** basic analytics (pages visited, approximate location, device/browser) and any cookies you accept — see the cookie note below.

**4. Why we use it, and our legal basis**

- To respond to your enquiry and provide the service — *performance of a contract* / *steps prior to a contract*.
- To send you service-related messages and, where permitted, occasional updates about ReviewCatcher — *legitimate interests* or *consent*, with an opt-out in every message.
- To meet tax and legal obligations — *legal obligation*.
- To understand and improve our website — *consent* (analytics cookies) or *legitimate interests*.

**5. Cookies**
We use only essential cookies by default. Any analytics or non-essential cookies load only if you accept them via our cookie banner. You can withdraw consent at any time in your browser settings.

**6. Who we share data with**
We use a small number of trusted service providers ("processors") to run the service — for example website hosting, email, SMS delivery and scheduling/automation tools. We share only what's needed, under contract, and never sell your data. A current list is available on request. Some providers may process data outside the EEA; where they do, we rely on appropriate safeguards (such as an adequacy decision or Standard Contractual Clauses).

**7. How long we keep it**
We keep enquiry and client data only as long as needed for the purpose it was collected and to meet legal/tax obligations, then delete it. Data we process on behalf of a client is kept only for the life of that engagement and deleted or returned when it ends.

**8. Your rights**
You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time. To exercise any of these, contact us at support@reviewcatcher.ie. You also have the right to complain to the Data Protection Commission (**dataprotection.ie**).

**9. Changes**
We may update this policy; the "last updated" date shows the latest version.

## Compliance Statement

**Last updated: [date]**

ReviewCatcher is built to keep both us and our clients on the right side of the rules. This statement summarises the standards we work to.

**Data protection (GDPR & Irish Data Protection Acts).**
We handle all personal data in line with the EU General Data Protection Regulation and the Irish Data Protection Acts. When we process a client's customer data, we do so only as a processor, under a signed Data Processing Agreement, strictly on the client's instructions.

**Electronic marketing (ePrivacy).**
Messages we send on a client's behalf comply with Ireland's ePrivacy Regulations (S.I. 336/2011). We only contact individuals for whom the client has a lawful basis — typically existing customers who provided their details and were given the chance to opt out — and every message includes a simple opt-out (e.g. reply STOP), which we honour immediately.

**Google review guidelines.**
Our method complies with Google's review policies. We ask every customer the same way, with no incentives and no "gating" (we never filter out unhappy customers or ask only happy ones). This keeps our clients' review profiles genuine and within Google's rules.

**Security.**
Personal data is stored in access-controlled systems, encrypted in transit, and reachable only by the tools needed to deliver the service. We keep processing records and review our providers for appropriate safeguards.

**Questions or concerns** about our compliance can be raised at support@reviewcatcher.ie; data-protection concerns can also be escalated to the Data Protection Commission (dataprotection.ie).

## AI & Automation Statement

**Last updated: [date]**

We believe in being upfront about how we use automation and artificial intelligence, in line with the transparency principles of the EU AI Act and GDPR.

**Where we use AI and automation.**
ReviewCatcher uses automation to schedule and send review-request messages, and uses AI to help draft replies to reviews. This is what lets us deliver the service reliably and at a price small businesses can afford.

**Human oversight.**
AI assists our work; it doesn't run unsupervised. Review responses and message templates are set up and overseen by a person, and clients can review or change how their responses are handled at any time.

**No significant automated decisions.**
We do not use AI to make solely automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects about any individual (GDPR Article 22). AI here drafts and schedules ordinary customer communications — nothing more.

**Your data and AI.**
We do not permit personal data handled through our service to be used to train third-party AI models. AI tools are used only to generate the specific output needed to deliver the service.

**Talk to a human.**
Anyone who receives a message from us can reply to reach a person, and any client can ask for a human to handle any part of their account.

Questions about our use of AI? Contact support@reviewcatcher.ie.
